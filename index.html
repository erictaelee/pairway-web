Our mission

To build the world's most connected golf community, making great company the standard for every round.

Golf is a social game, but finding people who match your pace, skill level, and schedule is still mostly luck. A friend of a friend, a lucky pairing at the club, a post in a Facebook group. We built Pairway Golf to make that connection using AI. Tell us how you play and who you'd like to play with, and we'll do the matching, so every tee time starts with a group that actually fits.

How it works

Matched in four simple steps.

  1. 01

    Build your golfer profile

    Share your handicap, home course, and a bit about how you like to play. Casual weekend rounds or competitive stroke play.

  2. 02

    Set your preferences

    Dial in what matters to you: pace of play, age range, availability, and the kind of playing partner you're looking for.

  3. 03

    Get matched

    Pairway Golf's AI engine analyzes your pace, vibe, and schedule to surface nearby golfers you'll genuinely click with. Browse personalized recommendations, connect with confidence, and start building your crew.

  4. 04

    Meet on the course

    Chat, lock in a tee time, and head out for a round with a group that actually fits your game.

About us

Started on the first tee, waiting for a group.

Pairway Golf started with a simple, familiar problem: showing up to a course solo and hoping to get paired with people you'd actually enjoy playing with. We figured the golf community deserved a better way to find each other, one built specifically around how golfers actually think about a good round: skill level, pace, and personality.

We're a small team of golfers and builders working and leveraging AI to bring that idea to life, starting with the Pairway moblie app.
