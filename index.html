Smart matching

Matches that work both ways.

Pairway doesn't just show you everyone nearby. It finds the golfers you'd enjoy playing with, who'd enjoy playing with you too, and puts the best fit first.

It has to fit you both

Your game is checked against what they're looking for, and theirs against yours. A match only counts if it works for both of you.

Free when you are

Only golfers whose free time overlaps yours make the list, so every match is a round you can actually play.

Ranked, not ruled out

Your must-haves are respected. Everything else, like pace and handicap, shapes the order, so a great partner who differs on one thing still makes your list.

What we look at

  • Skill level
  • Pace of play
  • When you're free
  • Distance
  • Age range
  • Who you'd like to play with
  • Drinking on the course
  • Smoking on the course

Safe by design

Play with people you can trust.

  • Chat only when you both say yes

    No one can message you unless you've liked each other.

  • Your details stay private

    Golfers see your first name and roughly how far away you are, never where you live.

  • Adults only

    Pairway is for golfers 18 and over.

  • Block or report anytime

    Unmatch, block or report anyone in a couple of taps.

Our mission

To build the world's most connected golf community, making great company the standard for every round.

Golf is a social game, but finding people who match your pace, skill level, and schedule is still mostly luck. A friend of a friend, a lucky pairing at the club, a post in a Facebook group. We built Pairway Golf to make that connection on purpose. Tell us how you play and when you're free, and our smart matching does the rest, so every tee time starts with a partner who actually fits.

About us

Started on the first tee, waiting for a group.

Pairway Golf started with a simple, familiar problem: showing up to a course solo and hoping to get paired with people you'd actually enjoy playing with. We figured the golf community deserved a better way to find each other, one built specifically around how golfers actually think about a good round: skill level, pace, and personality.

We're a small team of golfers and builders bringing that idea to life, starting with the Pairway mobile app.
